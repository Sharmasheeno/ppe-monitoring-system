# Code of Conduct

We want Sentinel to be a welcoming project. Contributors are expected to:

- Be respectful in issues, PRs and discussions.
- Assume good intent.
- Give constructive feedback — critique the code, not the person.
- Accept that maintainers may decline contributions that don't fit the roadmap.

Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory language will not be tolerated.
Report incidents by emailing the maintainer at abdisharmahassan@gmail.com.
